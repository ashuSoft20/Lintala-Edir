How Our Idir Works

A simple system based on shared responsibility and community cooperation.

1

Member Contributions

Members contribute according to association rules.

2

Collective Purchasing

Resources are used to purchase shared equipment.

3

Community Support

Members request equipment when ceremonies or emergencies happen.

4

Sharing and Returning

Equipment is recorded, used, and returned for other members.

About Our Idir

Idir is a traditional Ethiopian community-based association founded on unity, cooperation, mutual assistance, and shared responsibility. Our goal is to bring members together so that nobody faces important life events or difficult times alone.
